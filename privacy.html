Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Clip Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What data it uses

  • With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, it requests the YouTube scopes youtube.upload (to upload videos) and youtube.readonly (to read the name of the authorized channel).
  • The resulting OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share any personal data.
  • It does not access accounts of anyone other than the owner.
  • It uses no analytics, cookies or tracking.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
